All human languages for all human knowledge

Published: 2024-08-13 • Original link

https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06097

I hope you all will work on “all human languages for all human knowledge”.  
 
For the Internet, that is the recommendation. But it needs people to do it right, once and for all. It is possible, and it would greatly help stabilize and open the AI input data process so that it can be seen, verified and improved. Putting knowledge into universally accessible form also simplifies integration and exploration, but not by putting control in the hands of a few.
Please do not forget there are countless “domain specific languages” that affect every industry, profession, group, and future for human kind. Besides “all human languages” that we have inherited.
Translating Science Technology Engineering Mathematics Computing Finance Government Corporate Organizational terms, practices and methods is also needed, more.
 
It is possible.  AI companies and AIs can be required to help, not just make money for a few.
 
Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
