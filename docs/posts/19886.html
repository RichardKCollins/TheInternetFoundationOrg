You pushed whole new technologies, story lines, ethical concerns, complex issues, into one story. If you would have stopped when you got the story written and taken it and carefully laid out related stories, you could even abandon (set aside) this one story or keep it as a master, write all the related stories and then put it back. Tens of stories on human to android body stories, dozens of corporation and group conflicts, many stories of human androids living and interfacing with still more complex systems. You can take human memories and put them into many people. Just take Nakamura’s Japanese ability and cultural data and simply add it to any other human/android.

All I am recommending here is that every time you write a story that has this many new technologies and issues STOP. Analyze, document, design and prepare several of the related story lines and do them later or first. You have enough ideas for tens of thousands of stories and issues. Be methodical, write down your ideas, keep it all organized and produce it all, or make places for others to help.

You are making good characters and having them interact in action sequences that naturally flow. That makes for good individual stories. But you are also creating new concepts and civilizations, new technologies and industries and futures. Document them. Then write dozens of stories creating new unique characters in those frameworks.

Don’t hurry to crank out one action story with two good characters,. Write the story, STOP, study the characters, write new stories for those characters. Study the issues and technologies — write more stories for those with new characters. EVERY story you write STOP, analyze, extract stories and document them. Then simply pick story projects off the LONG list of documented ones — and not have to make them up one by one.

A story idea creates 10 or 20 story lines and possible new civilizations or industries or technologies or issues or moral dilemmas — each one can have many stories. Just keep a database or list and make them as you have time, it does not have to be in any particular order, if you have all of them documented from where they start.
