Universal Energy I first ran into these in the 1970s, the term then most often used was “chemical clocks” But also https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belousov%E2%80%93Zhabotinsky_reaction

Ilya Prigogine at UT Austin and others were looking at it from the point of view of systems of chemical reactions far from equilibrium. That is what Prigogine got his Nobel Prize for soon after. It is spatially oscillating. You see the spirals of galaxies? They are sometimes called “self organizing”. Wind blown sand. There are many emergent patterns.

It is not hard to model these with simulations. Plasma dynamics, waves and structures, resonances, stable states, chemical thermodynamics, development in embryos. There is a rather extensive literature and activities. The earliest I saw things like that was in chemistry classes about 1963 when many of the notions of states in spectroscopy were emerging because it had become possible to use computers to calculate these things precises. Setting patterns then solving for how to make them by controlling boundaries and flows is possible now. Most any of LLM AIs can help get started. But there are many other approaches to working with “all knowledge” in a domain like that now.  It is a large field and much has not been consolidated or organized.

It can be used in chemical engineering at scale to make new processes, petro-chemistry, flame chemistry, biochemistry, photonics, plasma chemistry., controlled thermonuclear processes will likely make their final breakthrough from using many computers to solve to stable states. Including fusion propulsion and surface wave propulsion in air, water and plasma.
