I have written elsewhere about making materials with bonds in the KeV to MeV range. I call them “atomic fuels” or “extended nuclear materials. The “chemical” bonds are generally less than 10 electron volts (eV). A fuel with 10 Kev = 10,000 electron volt bond would have an energy density 1000 times greater than chemical fuels. To put that in perspective, that 300 meter tall SpaceX fuel tank (essentially all fuel and oxygen) would shrink to 1 meter.. I took my first chemistry class as freshman in high school in 1964 when my Dad was working at Cape Canaveral. We all wanted to get to the Moon. I hated (still do) the belching chemical rockets and wanted to make atomic fuels that were safe and 100% efficient. I went to many universities and worked at places to find what was needed. That include 55 years of electrodynamics, gravitation, detection of dynamic gravitational fields, nuclear fusion, and nuclear structure and reactions of all isotopes.

It is 60 years now. And I understand it well enough to design most anything I want.
