Pretty much everything you want to do with molecules, can be done with atoms and particles, but 1000 times higher energy density. The Internet is moving that way steadily, but it can go faster and more deliberately, and more efficient for global society – intentionally, rather than everyone reinventing “how to get humans to work together” at all scales. Chains and networks of atoms, rather than bulky molecules.
