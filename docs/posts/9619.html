Published: 2023-05-26 • Original link

Flyment: How Qatar Airways Secretly wanted to Circumvent landing rights to Dominate the Market at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RL1RxSK-UGA

Flyment, You outlined some of the mistakes they made, and probably other airlines make. But you did not research solutions and pathways for the global industry. Much of air flight is unnecessary, and stems from too many airline investors chasing easy profits, when transportation is hard work. A hypersonic vehicle can go orbital, or stay atmospheric. I think Elon Musk will try StarShip on global point to point human and freight markets. An “orbital” route, might add a few extra hours and bring premium prices, at least until people get bored with that too. Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
