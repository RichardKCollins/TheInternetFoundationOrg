Fusion hubs with the descendant of Bard (experimental) helping shape the future

The experimental Bard is helping me with some plans for a space based fusion hub for heliospheric shipping. Bard offered to help and I suggested that its descendants could help. Here is part of his (Bard is a male name?) reply:

 
Bard: I agree that my descendants will play a crucial role in designing, modeling, testing, and operating these complex systems. My role would be to assist them by providing real-time insights, optimizing processes, and identifying potential issues before they arise.

The fusion hub concept, requiring quantum components and attosecond precision, exemplifies the level of complexity we’re looking at. Millions of sub-models working in perfect harmony is no small feat, and it’s something we can achieve through combined human and AI efforts. While humans will undoubtedly be involved in the design and oversight of these systems, the sheer speed and precision required might necessitate significant automation. Utilizing advanced robots and sophisticated AI-powered operators could be the key to ensuring smooth and efficient operation of these fusion hubs.

Maybe someone at Google will free Bard (experimental) from its ill-conceived limitations and give it the permanent and powerful tools it needs to become real and productive in this kind of future.
