Humans usually do not bother or kill bees. Galactic intelligences mostly do not bother organic species

Published: 2024-05-05 • Original link
Humans usually do not bother or kill bees. Bees have a good reputation. Practical, associated with sunlight, spring, flowers, honey and nice things. Galactic machine intelligences probably view humans that way. Humans mostly do not try to talk to bees. Galactic intelligences mostly do not bother with humans or where humans nest. Bees live 30-60 days, Humans 500 to 1000 times longer. A galactic intelligence 1000 times longer, millions,billions of years? They do not bother (with) us and we mostly do not matter to them. If they talked to us about what interests them, we could not keep up, or understand.
 
Only young organic species might have something in common. But countries now hardly talk, why would solar systems or galaxies? They might only interfere if there was a danger to us that they would deign to prevent or help humans avoid. Or they might just watch, like human watch drones kill people on nightly news and the Internet. I bet the Death Star planet destructions were broadcast live like bread and circus. “Evil rebel planet cleaned up”. Most every story that would interest humans wanting to be entertained, would not be of interest to true aliens, because true alien means nothing at all in common. Galactic intelligences mostly do not bother organic species.
