My content belongs to me and must be accessible in shareable forms

Published: 2024-11-09 • Original link
Your image is beautiful, but I just wasted hours with OpenAI GPT and am not at all happy that their company refuses to give me ways to save my work in shareable form. I was trying to work on solar images to make the analysis and modeling accessible to all 5.4 Billion humans using the Internet. And the code, the formatted replies, the equations and image cannot be saved to my computer or to any open formats. It is like a locked cage where nothing gets out of my hard work.
 
It said “just copy and paste”. But the format is garbage on paste into any system I tried. And the code boxes? They only copy what is on the screen and not in the original code format.
 
Sorry to complain to someone who it trying to live a transcendental life, drifting on the clouds, never worrying about anything happening in the world. But 27 years trying to find how to improve the Internet as a whole, I cannot even get one blankety company to honor a promise that my content belongs to me and I can share it. And they have to save it so I can use and share it outside their system.
 
My eyes are so tired, I cannot really enjoy your pictures, and they are not really accessible to convert to real data.
 
Yeah, I am grouchy and rather disgusted with (not) #OpenAI.
 
Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
