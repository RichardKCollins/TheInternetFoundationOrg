Parametric control system optimization makes many tiny corrections in real time — modeling the response of the air and nearby structures, and the whole vehicle. Which can be measured and modeled now. The models for today’s rockets and jets ignores the response of the atmosphere (a big part of the cost, and critical to hypersonic). And ignores the molecular responses of the air at high frequency.
