\[
\mathbf{B} = -\nabla \Phi
\]
– The condition \(\nabla \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0\) (Gauss’s law for magnetism) ensures that the scalar potential satisfies Laplace’s equation:

3. **Solution Procedure:**
– By solving Laplace’s equation with the specified boundary conditions, the magnetic field is reconstructed in the region between the photosphere and the source surface.
