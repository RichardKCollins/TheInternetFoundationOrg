Yeah, I started the Internet Foundation in July 1998 after the original Foundation was cancelled. And a few weeks before Google was incorporated. Today its budget would be about $6 billion per year. The VP at Network Solutions asked me why I would take on something with that kind of requirements, but no resources? I said “better one person than no one at all”. 27 years at least 360 days a years, and 12 – 18 hours a day is over 110,000 hours. I managed to structure the Internet and lay out plans for its future, and write guidelines on AIs and country management. Set some good policies and review many hundreds of special cases for groups and types of organizations. I think I will stop in a few more years. But with Grok and GPT and Google AI and others, I can squeeze out a few of the more serious problems with 5.6 billion Internet users and many social media sites who are not serving their customers well yet. I mostly review posts on X and write about issues – suggesting different ways to look at global problems and different ways to look at industries, technologies, science and all human knowledge.

I accept viewing and reading many thousands of pages each week. My writing could never capture a fraction of what I see and remember. Most people do not read what I write, but I feel I should at least try to write some down, if for nothing else to clarify things by writing – that won’t happen just by thinking. Pretty sure YT does not limit comments. If they did, I keep notes elsewhere too. A few more years and my life will be over, I won’t be here to see it all erased and forgotten, but I will feel better because I wrote it down. At least some AI will remember fragments, perhaps.

Many of the groups might build websites, post materials, raise funds, even give out money, assign staff and volunteers to help with things they know. Then everyone gives up without due diligence or sufficient effort. Many times solutions simply are not in the minds of the people assigned.
