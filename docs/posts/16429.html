Is the division between India and Bangladesh primarily religious, ethnic and language? Or it is just a jumble of historical periods of dominance by others and arbitrary geographic boundaries?

Microsoft Bing CoPilot:

The division between India and Bangladesh is a complex interplay of several factors, including religious, ethnic, linguistic, and historical influences.

  1. Religious: The partition of British India in 1947 was primarily based on religious lines, creating India (with a Hindu majority) and Pakistan (with a Muslim majority). Bangladesh was initially part of Pakistan, known as East Pakistan12.
  2. Ethnic and Linguistic: Ethnic and linguistic differences also played a significant role. The people of East Pakistan (now Bangladesh) were predominantly Bengali-speaking, while the dominant language in West Pakistan (now Pakistan) was Urdu. This linguistic divide contributed to cultural and political tensions2.
  3. Historical: The history of the region is marked by periods of dominance by various empires and colonial powers, including the British. The arbitrary geographic boundaries drawn by the British during the partition did not always align with the ethnic and linguistic realities on the ground12.
  4. Political: The political and economic marginalization of East Pakistan by the central government in West Pakistan led to growing discontent and eventually the Bangladesh Liberation War in 1971, resulting in the independence of Bangladesh2.

In summary, the division between India and Bangladesh is not solely based on one factor but is a result of a combination of religious, ethnic, linguistic, and historical influences.
