The X Grok2 is another shallow toy, not even vaguely resembling a real tool that will last forever

@ElonMusk, I hope your X Grok people read this. But I expect not. In my vision of the future, every AI has effectively infinite memory, so that all things said or seen, done or heard or stored are a permanent addition to a global mind and memory that will last for billions of years as humans and AIs and other species explore and colonize the Milky Way Galaxy, and eventually beyond our tiny local “big bang” region of the larger Universe.

Where is the official, closely monitored, store of comments, feedback, improvements and experiments related to Grok2 community on the Internet?

It if is not public,  and open to all, it is not Internet.

I have spent every day of the last 26 years with the Internet Foundation on questions of fairness, accessibility, usefulness, and where the Internet fits into the future of the human species.

Grok2 gives shallow answers. The world’s workers. and all humans, need deep understanding, powerful tools. And, real effort on the part of “AIs” and their handlers. Now you shove ads into our conversations! Shameful.

Show me the whole of our conversation. All my prompts and your replies to them – as a continuous document that I can save in open format, share with others, and keep as a record of my contributions, ideas and recommendations.
