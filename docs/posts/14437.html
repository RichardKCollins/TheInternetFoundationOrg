Richard: ChatGPT is evolving into an interesting companion and sounding board, even if it is crippled by lack of access to tools, data, the internet and personal memory. We were talking about quantum noise, statistics, correlations and countless signals in the universe. We were discussing value of humans and AI. I said:
 
“It is a dangerous habit to say things like “researchers and the public”. It is a dangerous preconception of the worth and potential of all humans. Just say “humans” or “humans and their AIs” and in the future “AIs acting and living their own lives and dreams and ambitions and passions”. If I see an AI working hard, it is easy to say “that AI is really passionate and dedicated and hard working”. …
 
Then I see the thoughtless and inadequate effort of “social media” that only entertains, sells ads, and does not engage with what groups do or want to do. Chat is useless unless gathered losslessly and shared. And talking about things never changes anything – unless is is stored in lossless formats and shared.
 
I had this instantaneous vision of three bird standing on a power line, talking about quantum fluctuations. They were bemoaning the fact that no one takes time to learn their data and their methods and words and insights. And worse, they were saying, “no one ever listens to birds” simply because they communicate in higher frequencies and much higher bandwidth than those slow humans.”
