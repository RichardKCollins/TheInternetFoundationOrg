Global open sharing of progress with AI groups

Published: 2023-07-25 • Original link

Is there a progress report for Bard that show what is happening for the whole Bard project? I would like to see how it is going. I do not have time to create a summary for myself by aggregating stuff from the web. And I would hope that Google is keeping track of all the many people and what Bard has learned. But I have not seen any of the “AI” groups report how their products are doing. Nor any head-to-head comparisons and benchmarks. Just a statistical summary with things like “In all conversations, the number of times human express that the AI was wrong and the total number of conversations.” “In all conversations, the number of times human offer suggestions and ideas and a list and count of the them.”

I was more suggesting that if AIs are supposed to be partners with the human species for the good of all, then all AI groups should publicly share how well their AIs are learning. Not as advertising exaggeration, but more like a standard report. My educated guess is that the lessons learned and the best practices for all AIs are being duplicated and repeated by all AI groups. Each AI group interactions with humans, the value of the information and experience depends on the background of the humans, their skills, the difficulty of the problems discussed, and the effort expended. There are thousands of groups trying to make “AIs” and they all are reinventing the wheel. And they are all hoarding knowledge about the whole, that grasping is probably hurting the progress toward true human capable AIs for the good of all humans.

I can easily suggest a way forward. Google is one of the more visible actors. If they would choose a neutral party, then they can support global standards and open sharing toward fully human capable AIs. Across not only AIs but many database, information system and other efforts at gathering and indexing all human knowledge where humans can query in human languages. Efforts like wikipedia, github, even Twitter and YouTube all are trying to gather, facilitate and encourage collaboration where there are millions even billions of humans sharing and working for the good of all. Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation

I recommend SI units, global fundamental constants and reference, symbolic mathematics verifiers and stepping, computer language debug and testing. Simulations and animations. Just make “working with Bard Chat” a powerful tool for work on anything, at any level of complexity, and with groups as large as needed.

I read hundreds of papers each month in science technology engineering mathematics computing finance governance and other quantitative topics. Many of them have equations, tables of data, links to data streams, links to videos, links to github and sharing sites, links to other papers and books. All that needs to be “real”. Rather than equations flattened into text on the screen that has to be read by eyes, the equations can be in symbolic form and opened in symbolic math verifiers and toolkits. The basics are not hard. Same with data, charts, visualizations, diagrams, process models, financial reports and plans, instructions, guidelines, and many types of content. Computer languages, whole computer projects and package source codes. Each task can have best in world tools. Bard Chat becomes not a static product that one company sells, but Google support for tools that people use. The Google Chrome browser is a software package. it needs to be part of a service that is more flexible to meet the needs of all humans in all their many occupations and life tasks. Including humans from birth to the end of their life and all things in between. And humans in groups of all sizes.

It looks like the Google Bard interface to all human knowledge is improving. Tell the Team “Thanks, and keep up the good work!” for me. Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation

On the question I started with about text to animated video. I think that is one of many kinds of “skills” that Bard could have. It might mean using an app that someone wrote as a Google Chrome extension. But an AI ought to be able to take a request like “Please make this conversation into an animated video using my default voices and characters, or let me select new voices and characters”.

Setting the content of the shared knowledge has an added benefit. Then the content types will gradually all be recorded and globally accessible to all Internet users. Actually, in reference to “I also agree that the AI should suggest ways to proceed, rather than trying to program it.” what I intended was that your Bard Team should let Bard lay out alternatives and itself choose a good path. Have it suggest a plan for what to do, then have Bard do the programming needed. Humans are really hard to teach where memorization and many complex and exact steps have to be followed. So do not waste time, rather just have Bard do the programming and run the computer for the human. Only about 1 in a 1000 of humans will be interested in the details. They will ask for tools to do that sort of thing, One of the content types is “instructions for automated processes”. That “Save this conversation to antimated video (with my defaults choices for voices and characters) and send a link to my “Close Friends” lists”

Not quite there yet. Let Bard be able to generate suggestions and then follow up the suggestion itself. Let Bard follow the suggestions it would say to the human and write the program itself.

You are generating a lot of suggestions that put the burden back on the human. People ask about software, NOT to write software, but to get the computer to do something. So Bard might say now “it would be good idea to write a program like ____” but it could just as well read the suggestion before saying it to the human, and Bard write the program and run it.

Bard is doing the same thing, putting the burden on the human. Bard might suggest, “Go look for things on the Internet”. But Bard could “look for things on the Internet, record that as part of the conversation content, summarize it and save that as part of the content of the collaboration session.”
