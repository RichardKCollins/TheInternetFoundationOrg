Could you explain what is going on in AutoStakkert when it is finding 10% of 5000 frames? I have a vague idea, but you are using it. What does it emphasize and what do you think is happening there? I do not have a telescope. I use data from the Internet. So I never see the live data or the processing. I have no real idea of the exposures, frame rate and time to take 5000 frames. There seem to be a range of Apollo cameras using Sony IMX432, IMX429 and IMX174 with frame rates maximum 126 fps, 85 fps, and 164 fps respectively. And faster for smaller fields. The resolutions are 1608*1104, 1944*1472, 1936*1216. I write things out so I remember them. The Apollo website shows nice pictures but does not say how hard it is to make the pictures and how much time to gather and process them.

Thank you,
Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
