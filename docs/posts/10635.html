Published: 2023-07-26 • Original link

Richard:

We talked before a bit about teaching AIs to use software, rather than trying to write it. There are a few ten million humans out of the 5 Billion using the Internet who try to write software, scripts and automation. Forcing billions of humans to become programmers, just to use computers is not an efficient strategy. Now these “language AIs” are pre-compiling collections of text. In fact, the language AIs, with a pre-compiled statistical index of a “large” text corpus to give them the essentials of the language, can then be assigned to index any body of text.

The AI has “language abilities” because it has access to the pre-compiled “language model”. What it also gets in terms for general knowledge right now is a somewhat random process, depending on what text training sets were thrown into the model beyond the minimum required to learn the language rules. When a “competent language model”, based on its training set and pre-compilation method is given additional text to compile, it is essentially a language capable indexer of the material. You can ask a “competent language model AI” questions about the material and it will answer well or poorly, depending on how well the total training materials are structured to provide verifiable testable competency in a topic or skill.

Suppose you want to have an “Accounting AI”. It would required the module for the human language(s) it needs to communicate, and background on organizations. Sort of the material any human accountant would learn from growing up in human society, going through the school system, playing with friends and peers, watching videos, playing games, interacting with others, reading books and other human occupations and life events. That probably includes interacting with family, some events like wedding funerals parties church gathering. My point here it that a “competent Accounting AI” in human form will be expected to be well rounded, and to have lived a “decent human life” so that it would know how to react, as a human would, in its interactions with its co-workers, human and AI.

So this method of picking huge piles of unstructured uncurated unindexed unverified text to train the “language AI” in general human skills and abilities is WRONG. A human child, these is some effort by human society to makes sure the curriculum is balanced and fairly complete. A human child is expected, and tested somewhat, on its ability to reason in certain ways, to have a competency in arithmetic, counting, mathematics, English (or local language), science, history. The curriculum standards are somewhat agreed upon, but currently NOT global or universal. Even the collection of all curriculum at pre-kindergarten, kindergarten, grade school, middle school, high school, trade school, undergraduate, and graduate training systems in the world is NOT indexed and curated and complete. I know, I checked over the last 25 years of the Internet Foundation.

So, if you want a well trained “Accounting AI”, you take a language AI worth of pre-compiled and certified language skills and add the skills for arithmetic, counting, reporting, organization, ledgers, and all the things you would test human accountants on. If a human comes to apply for the job, they will be tested on their ability. Usually we check to see what schools (training sets) they are trained on. How well they did on the tests and assignments. What their teachers and former employers said about them. An “Accounting AI” MUST have its competency measured in a globally accessible form.

Which gets to “AI competency in using software”. If you hire someone at Google, I bet they all mostly have to know computer languages, have done well in school, have worked at jobs where they would get various useful competencies, write well, understand and communicate well. An AI can be tested and judged for jobs the same way. You should treat the AI as fully human. NOT that it has the same shape, or is made of organics or not, is using the same DNA as humans, went to the same schools as humans – but that it can do the job well, and be adaptable for future work. You want “competent and good AIs” , the same way you want “competent and good human coworkers and collaborators”.

So you might want some that can manipulate human written computer codes, and be able to read the documentation for various software (particularly open software projects now) and be able to use that for the purposes of the organization, within the context of a country perhaps, and within the context of the human species survival and “the good of all humans and related species”.

Humans and AIs treating all AIs as “human”, is a formal way for all AIs to be judged according to their abilities at tasks and assignments, but also as competent and good workers in organizations for organizational goals, for competency and correct behavior in countries and in global human and AI society. If humans are expected to judge AIs by a fragmented system of local values, that is too hard, nearly impossible to verify manage audit summarize teach.

Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
