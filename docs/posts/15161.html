Give AIs large mandates, not narrow computer tasks

Published: 2024-05-08 • Original link
Give true AIs large mandates, not narrow computer tasks – For the cost of one human position, you can install continuously improving global open resource nodes. Not just change one human’s life, but all 8.1 Billion.  Find, gather, verify, curate, summarize, share, connect, predict, test, compare, enable — good computer learning and sharing will impact and likely improve all your efforts, your group, your country, your networks, and the world.
 
Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
