*Please map all groups, topics, opportunities on the Internet with global open methods.
*Let anyone use and add knowledge.
*Map, animate and edit ALL things so humans and AIs can see and interact with real things, not just read text about them.
*The “global open” part records, tokenizes, verifies and illuminates the contributors, content, purposes, stakeholders, methods, and all affected.
*Make all groups index their sources and methods, and use global, open, lossless, verifiable, auditable, accessible formats – that can be immediately mapped and simulated using ‘best on Internet’ open tools.
