You should read my book, The First Summer, at https://www.amazon.com/First-Summer-Danas-Story/dp/B09RLY9CBK

The second and third books are about true machine intelligence – where mind to mind communication is facilitated, recorded, combined and shared. Most all the technologies already exist to increase machine-human communication bandwidth many powers of 1000. Immersive experiences using machines is possible now. But I started out because of how our family and certain humans can do this on their own. In the Summer book, Dana and her sister work it out on their own, but it quickly spreads.

When Joe Weber showed me his gravitational wave detector, I asked him what he wanted to use it for. And he said “for 3D gravitational wave communication” – where distance does not matter, and there is no ambiguity. I instantly knew what he meant. So there is likely a basis for what you are talking about.  It would have high survival value. And it should be possible to measure and reproduce it.
