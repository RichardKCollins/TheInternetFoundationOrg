Potential ( 2.99792458E8 meters/second)^2 = 8.98755179E16 (Joules/Kilogram) seems impossible.

But, the vacuum is non-linear as one approaches the speed of light and gravity. I grew up with everyone laughing at the fools who said it was impossible to break the sound barrier. But, then we used the nonlinear (compressible) fluid equations for real gases, understood what we were facing, and just did it. We also created new engines and technologies and materials no one had before. So “the impossible” became routine and “off-the-shelf”.
