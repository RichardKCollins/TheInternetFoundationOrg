Comment on another exoskeleton boast

Published: 2024-10-30 • Original link

Use the muscles that people have. With sensors and controls that need not be bulky or take a lot of power. My dream since my brother broke his neck 37 years ago. He was completely paralyzed and on a respirator, but none of his muscles changed. Just no way to stimulate them, take feedback and monitor. Not a hard computer problem now. Please do it. At least make a breathing algorithm. We do hearts, why not breathing, walking, running, dancing, riding a motor cycle, living a regular life?
