Focus on “heliospheric exploration”, “mapping fields and interiors”, “catalyzing new technologies”, “solar system colonization” and “a full complete digital twin of the earth (and sun) and its fields.” There is a lot to do, but it is NOT hard, just tedious. Smart people might help, but much of it is “doing a good job”, “helping others”, “helping all humans, not just a few”. Attract people who care about relevance and supporting solar system technologies and planning. Let your whole university help and have the gameboard open to everyone, all humans and their AIs. And all schools down to kindergarten of all sorts. It can use supercomputers, but they have to be adapted to support global collaboration, NOT a few brute force projects that no one can access or use. It is not for the fame of a few, but to extend the human species into the whole of the heliosphere and beyond. Solar system wide base lines can extend views of the sun and other suns many orders of magnitude with distributed systems and open collaborations with all countries, not just the rich and pushy ones. Please stop thinking so narrowly. People are a dime a dozen now. Even Nobel prize winners are common and mostly ignored. But true global (or heliospheric) open real time collaborative networks that really focus on service to all humanity do not really exist yet. You can test it and see what happens. It has to be transformative, open, verifiable, auditable, reliable, trustworthy and focus on all humans and related species, not just the benefits of a few.
