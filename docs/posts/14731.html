Note to Space News Pod

Published: 2024-04-22 • Original link

SpaceX Starship Lunar Mission GIANT UPDATE at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZ_YQVBXjCA

Your are just starting out and maybe you are getting in a rut. I strongly encourage you to drop long talking head sequences and help humans around the world to see things. You do not have to entertain, or wow, or get hearts pumping. It is not an action movie or sports cast.

Take a day off. Really look at the solar system and the universe for the next 2000 years. It is all there. You do not need eyes to see it.

You are really young. You have decades ahead of you. What can you accomplish? What can the humans in ALL countries accomplish?

If you are just an announcer, sorry I said anything. If you can accomplish much, what can you encourage billions to do? What is truly worth striving for and why?

Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
