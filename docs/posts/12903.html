CASP CAPRI meeting should become a permanent global collaboration

Published: 2023-12-21 • Original link

Will you use Zoom or something similar to allow remote collaboration and discussion, recording and sharing? Events posted as online videos (with well crafted links, community and explanations) have far-reaching impacts, way beyond a few people who can afford nice vacations at company expense. I would make a video to show how CAPRI and CASP and related groups are not doing a good job of sharing their work on the Internet. And not getting full benefit from the hard work everyone is putting in.

CASP CAPRI meeting should aim for a structured permanent global collaboration open to all 8 billion humans. Not many mostly closed small fragments on the Internet, not well linked nor accessible to the ultimate users and beneficiaries of your work. I will try to write it out, but no way can I afford to travel. I have followed CASP for some time, but there are lots of new things cooking on the Internet. You do not need an outsider to show you a portrait of your work and how it might affect the world, or maybe you do.

I will see if I can find all the ones who ought to be there and cannot afford expensive flights, hotels and such. But they and your groups can have a permanent worksite online that is open (visible) and accessible and affordable for all.

Thanks for your hard work and Merry Christmas!

Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
