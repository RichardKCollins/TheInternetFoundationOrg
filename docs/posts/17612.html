Still using low energy density chemicals when you could be using atomic fuels?

All that chemical mass for so little real energy or impulse. You ought to know better. Your belching engine is what one can you expect from a government agency afraid to tackle hard problems. You can go directly from nuclear and atomic to electric. You have not even tried hard.

 

https://www.facebook.com/reel/1078113573670585

The problem with sharing these solutions in video is finding the one(s) you need. Then you have to translate lots of pixels to another context. But you can make image algorithms to tokenize these instructions in a compact 3D animated form that carries all the information. Easier to match, easier to generalize and combine – at global scale for all people. And an AI assisted version can substitute local specifics for tokens in a 3D model, to fit to your situations. These videos actually give way too much information, in the wrong form for 8.2 billion humans.

Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
