Linus JC I have watched the Internet since before it started. It went from “its going to get better” to 5.4 Billion users and still “its going to get better”. Sharing videos is only a part of it. This person wanted to test an idea, measure temperature and velocity of air, and use a thermal camera. Maybe that will give him experience to optimize the rocket exhaust from the SpaceX booster one day. Not many of 5.4B people can afford his tools and materials, but they can see and consider his experiment. It ought to be indexed and accessible but the Internet is a jumble that Google has not properly indexed and shared back with the human species and the AIs. I am fairly certain he was not just thinking about heating rooms. Facebook does not give tools to select and control what is shown. They do not listen. Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
