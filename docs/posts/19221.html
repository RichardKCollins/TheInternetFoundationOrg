Yes, there are tiers to Grok services and subscriptions. Here’s a breakdown based on available information:

  • Free Tier: Grok is now available for everyone with certain limitations. Free users can ask up to 10 questions every two hours and have access to three image analyses and four image generations per day. This tier is intended to give users a taste of Grok’s capabilities without a financial commitment.

  • X Premium: This subscription tier provides access to Grok AI among other features. It costs $8 per month or $84 annually. Users with this subscription have increased access to Grok, allowing up to 50 questions per day.

  • X Premium+: The top-tier subscription, which is $16 per month or $168 per year, also includes access to Grok but with additional benefits like an ad-free experience on X and potentially faster access to new features. Premium+ subscribers get the full, unadulterated version of Grok.

These tiers are part of X’s strategy to make Grok accessible while encouraging users to subscribe for enhanced features and capabilities.
