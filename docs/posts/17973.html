Do lossless copies of the images from this group exist, for study, algorithm development? These are jpg (lossy) here and I do not want to waste any time playing with encoded and lossy data. Before processing, and tweaking.

If someone shared a small region of the sun at maximum magnification — EVEN if it does not appear clear – those could be smaller, high frame rate, high bit size images.

The Solar Dynamics Data is available in lossless formats, but the published Internet stuff is all lossy. I call it “eye candy”.

I have extra computers to do downloads and processes. If they have to run for days or weeks, OK.
