When you can see the enterprise as a living network of humans and things in a process, it can be continuously operated and improved for the good of all.

If I make a structure with building blocks, I can learn to manipulate blocks. But after many experience with structures the mind will move structures as easily as blocks. The mind does not care what shapes things are.

I first learned to work with forms, departments and procedures, then whole organisations and all things in them and connected, then regions and states, then industries and countries, projects and programs, then new industries, global and systemic issues. Now the whole world, all humans, all living things, all parts of the universe and all things in it.

But it is the same basic relationships between parts. The same geometries and movements. The same forces and costs and constraints. The same careful layout of connections. Size does not matter. “I have to build a story”, “I have to build a book”, “I have to build a library”, “I have to build an internet for a galactic civilization” — all the same , but different in size and number and the particular pieces. The behaviors, the actors, the conflicts the synergies, the joys and hopes.
