This is 911. Answer the following 7 random questions to prove you are human.

Published: 2024-08-09 • Original link

@elonmusk @OpenAI – This is 911. Answer the following 7 random questions to prove you are human.

Still not able to log into my account on OpenAI GPT Plus. I give my password correctly, then it forces me to “are you human” game of matching. There are 7 challenge problems, each that has several images to examine. It is NOT an efficient screen for humans at all. I cannot get it right because I refuse to play games that are not necessary.

 
I am fairly certain someone there wants to enslave human (paying customers) to fill in some ML algorithm they are playing with.
 
My setting was for email and text pin which is pretty much the standard on the Internet now. But they completely ignored that and put in this LONG game forcing people to play. I cannot log into my account at all. It let me change my password by sending email but not log in.

It is not nice to change the rules without informing and educating existing customers.  It is not nice to abuse and steal from older people, just because you seem to get away with it.

The person “helping me” by email, Shiena, “did you clear your cache and cookies”, “are you using VPN or incognito”. I think she has no clue what happens, there is no technical support. There is no human to talk to, and any human seems not trained or able to correct the bad decisions of someone else.
 
No one at OpenAI cares at all, no one pays attention. No one there has a clue what login is for.
 
The Internet Foundation could set policy for all sites on login.
 
Eventually it will take them weeks to do anything. They get paid by the hour. I will hire a 4th grader to play that game. But will it go away if I show I am human? I doubt it. OpenAI has slaves they can make do anything with no regrets. They are called “paying customers”.
 
Am I disgusted with that company? Yes, but they are holding hostage hundreds of long conversation I had. I need to log in at least once to get to my information, my ideas, my conversations. Difficult issues, many related to AI in human society. But some game player hidden in their system can manipulate indiscriminately without fear of discovery or punishment.
 
Send a damn pin like my bank and all the other systems. It works. And “are you human” at OpenAI does NOT.
 
I was going to talk to ChatGPT 4 o about supersonic shock wave equations and approaches. Those are easy. But my mind and my life are not geared to playing useless games. I can hold equations and data in mind indefinitely (at least 60 years), but useless patterns, not at all. Especially under duress, and so arbitrary.
 
But I am at the mercy of a human who does not know or care, and more humans there who do not know or care. Human life and time have value? They have no clue. What shameful activity from a company that ought to be perfect in all things, not always the last.
