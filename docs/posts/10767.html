Movies and images of the sun

Published: 2023-07-31 • Original link
The sun’s diameter is about 109 times the diameter of the earth.
This is a movie of today’s sun. Bottom right you can set the playback speed to 0.25 so you have time to look around.  I use a magnifying glass in Windows to look closely.  A lot happens.
https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/latest48.php?q=0171
These are snapshots of the sun today in different kinds of light.  Click on the blue “Data Links” and at the bottom of many it says “48 hour movie”. The ones that say 4096 are 4096×4096 images. When they open, click on them and it will zoom in.

https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/

If you are on Twitter, there are people I follow who post pictures of things happening on the sun
