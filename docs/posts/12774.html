Content based servers and user controlled viewers and user tools

Published: 2023-12-12 • Original link

Juxtapposed: I Redesigned the ENTIRE Steam UI from Scratch at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cDY2p1CTkPo

Nice work, but just one site and one topic. If all games on the Internet were handled by one game viewer (with all the things you listed and more), then the game suppliers and sales outlets would serve content for the “game viewer” and let the users choose the “game viewer” they like. In “rewrite the whole Internet”, that is the basic strategy. “global climate change” is one framework for the distributed data, all in open accessible formats. Then “best in world” viewers and visualization tools to query, merge, compare, combine, use that kind of content. Not be at the mercy of some newbie web designers, however smart they might be on a topic. Try Twitter, YouTube, Facebook, and some of the top 100 sized sites now. Automate your scanning of sites and characterization. Oh. Make one “cookie banner” and replace all the stupid (and often evil) ones on the Internet. Oh, do Amazon – they do a horrible job of product descriptions, categories, user support and user engagement. Oh. Do the “gov” domain which has lots of little “gov” domains for different countries. Please do the UN sites. Just do the whole internet. I sometimes call it “re-skinning the internet”, but once started, content based servers and user controlled viewers covers a lot. Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
