Edward Mehr,

Thank you for sharing your experiences and insights. Process and control systems optimization is critical to efficient global and heliospheric industries. When the methods are not refined and shared, individual groups might benefit, but reduce the potential of the human species. And its ability to serve 8.2 Billion humans and related species.

It might be sufficient to have a rule that all “data containers” must credit and bidirectionally link the original developers to global users.

When AI groups scrape from the Internet, they are copying content made by others. If a factory or process can be improved, opening its data in complete and lossless form, might be a way to invite independent and fresh views. Not just metals and forming, but chemical, nuclear, agriculture, biochemical, education, government, nuclear and atomic, social media, the Internet, AIs. There are many tens of thousands of global and systemic issues and opportunities that need improvements of scale and depth and efficiency.

“We will keep your information secure from your competitors” when the stakes are a few billion often pales when the stakes are trillions and affect all humans on earth. Then it tends to turn into “a local fight over turf in an industry” cripples new development and many humans die or live miserable lives.
 
Think about “Internet best practices”, “solar system development”, “material and process model calibration”, “Markets Products Industries Communities”, “Long range planning”, “open algorithm development”, “Process monitoring and control”, “Infrastructure Water Power Internet Food Weather”, “Global open resources.”
 
Most companies, industries, processes, countries, cities, systems on earth now are far below global optimum. A few see 98.5% and billions restricted to 1.5%.
 

For “solar system exploration and development” or “for the good of all humans and related species, including true AI species” what the world now needs is not more rats fighting in a box. Open the boxes, but track and encourage globally.
