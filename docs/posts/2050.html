Notes from Weather.gov survey questions.

Published: 2021-08-01 • Original link

Internet Research – global sensor networks and sharing

Looking for data to correlate with other sensor networks

Your left bars are rich in links. But you are using almost a different style of navigation on every page, certainly every section. With about 3 Million entry points for site:weather.gov that is a lot of hand building and maintaining. And a lot for users to absorb – and you keep changing. No clear sense of “lets all work together”

Your checkboxes here are not working. I checked on and it locked the others. I found broken links, had difficulty finding the technical details. And it is rather busy.

https://w2.weather.gov/climate/ has a broken link for Numerical Models. I would like to see all the Gov domains weather and climate information used with all the rest of the worlds data to image the atmosphere, interior of the oceans and earth, and all the fields (3D real time) shown as a whole.

Move Weather.gov to be an international agency working with the whole world, the whole Internet.

You should treat everyone as a friend and colleague – no matter their age or background.

Richard Collins, Director, The Internet Foundation
