All Internet providers could operate globally.

Published: 2024-04-26 • Original link

At https://broadbandnow.com/All-Providers it does not sort by max speed properly. Put Mbps (Megabitspersecond)) at the top, and just numbers in the column. Then make sure it works. Mega is uppercase.

The “Estimated Population Covered” is misleading since it is not customers. and you have no way to show multiple providers fighting for market share in each area.

I think the Internet provider rules need to change because we are a global world now.

Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
