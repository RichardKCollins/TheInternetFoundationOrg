Earth Fleet Commander’s Wife Was In Coma 22 Years — Until Dragon Lady’s Daughter Did The Impossible

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vquXkeTFCG4

My brother, Jeff, was in a coma for a month after he broke his neck at C2. He was completely paralyzed, not able to breath on his own, and in a coma. That was 1987 in northern Virginia near Washington DC. My Dad lived in Florida and it was hard for him to come. But I told my Dad, “When you come Jeff will know your voice and wake up”. That is exactly what happened. We were all at a family dinner, Jeff’s fiance and her family, and our family and friends. Dad went directly from the airport to the hospital. After dinner, when we went to the hospital as usual, Dad was there and Jeff was awake and aware. But with a tracheotomy blocking his air, he could not speak. So we found a lip reader and had them come and translate for us. After much effort the hospital put in a “talking trache” and we could hear better. Jeff lived for about two years. It was sad and horrible. It makes me sick to remember it.

I wrote a book (actually part of a trilogy) about a young girl, Dana, who learned to read minds. She and her sister could merge their minds then reach into the minds of people in comas and help them wake up. Many stories in the books about that. Including the Veterans hospital where Jeff finally died. It is possible, it is simply based on recognition and familiarity and excitation of familiar pathways linked to conversation.

You can find that book on Amazon “Dana, the First Summer” by Richard K Collins.

The second book is about Dana and Brian, who is an artificial intelligence. In the same way she was able to wake unconscious humans, she merged with a brain interface, machine learning system. It captured her patterns as the template for human core responses and language and all her feelings and skills. That template contained all the weights needed for mapping a human life and its skills. We know those kinds of things now as large language models or machine learning models. But this goes a bit further to mapping the response network of a human, or a society, or a body of knowledge. That was 10 years ago I wrote that. Still trying to create those interfaces which are almost possible now. It is not just money but something that seems to happen, when the time is right. You cannot force things. But, when it is time, it is time.

ow, the brain interface is all that is needed, the machine can read the full activity of the brain and find the patterns to wake up the human (or other brain). So whether humans can learn to read minds or not, the brain is accessible and can also be improved or repaired.

Big sacrifices and miracles are fun, but nothing is easy in life, nor are happy endings common. I keep saying to myself “this story is not real” and “what it describes is terms of change is mostly impossible”. Your heroes spend years or decades and then change. I have been working for 60 years now. The impossible of decades ago is possible now, except there is still no way to move beyond greed and self interest, to apply lessons known to work but dependent on human change. Good ideas are not done because they are right, they still take some inner change in each person’s life. And still one person can waste the efforts of hundreds of millions.

When I first started reading science fiction stories in the 1950’s they would have names like Xylprixon. I learned quickly to ignore the details and put in a place holder, get on with the story. I could hold 20 to 50 odd names and things and see the whole story. Over the decades I have read literally million of things where the name or details were unresolvable without a lot of effort. But by holding the pieces in their proper positions (based on sentence structure and framework) almost infinite numbers of dangling pieces could be handled. Now AIs and algorithms can do that for us. So if I read some random paper like “Semiclassical Partition Functions for Gravity with Cosmic Strings” I know it can be resolved, it just takes a bit of time. And the main purposes and implications do not have to wait for complete resolution or details.
