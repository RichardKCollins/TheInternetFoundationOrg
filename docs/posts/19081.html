Height, coverage, land use, sunlight, farming. It looks like you have lot of work left to do, to trace what to do about it, even if the method seems nice. Seems every problem I work on has 23 dimensions or more. (a figure of speech). The human eye and brain simply is not reliable to solve those, especially when the color combinations of the map have not been individually calibrated and tested. The human eye is not a very good tool for certain types of information processing, unless there is a way to extract the human response in a reliable way. and probably for many, or all, people.
 
Maybe some measure of “how unique is this?” or “so what?” or “do we need to do anything about that?” or “can this help change bad things?” or “can this help improve good things collectively enough so the result matters more the simultaneous bad ones?”
 
If you have difficulty reading this, ask your AI to discuss it with you. Sometimes interactive discussion is the only way to sort things out. And then there are problems that are so difficult they take decades to work out, no matter how beautiful the maps, or clear the vision.
