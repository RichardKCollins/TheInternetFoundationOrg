I wish I had this when I helped set up the Famine Early Warning System (FEWS.net) in 1986-1988. But I would not have used only images from the real world, rather comprehensive economic, social, technological, financial, trade, labor, family, organizational, meteorological, agricultural, governmental, systems, development, emergency, transport, medical, educational – whole world, whole country, whole city, whole topic, whole network, whole future – digital twins. We did on distributed microcomputer networks what could not be done on mainframes of that time – because it was open, sustainable, easy to learn and use. It is not hard to create and use multidimensional visualizations – with clear goals, real world metrics and priorities.

I would like to see you visualize ( site:un.org ) which has only 7.5 Million urls and a few billion elements that can be simplified without loss for visualizations that all humans can learn, use and apply to helping the world’s 8.1 Billion humans live lives with dignity and purpose.

You can trace links to related twins of all things in LLMs that use global open tokens – IDs of global open digital twins. Open, traceable, verifiable, can be used for UN metrics of global goals and priorities and results.

The twins can match many existing global topics and subjects so existing efforts are easy to find and understand in context. The STEMC-FGOT+ (science technology engineering mathematics computing finance governance organizations topics and more) groups are easy to integrate since their already are working. Gaps are easy to spot and global efforts simple to define for any missing or weak or closed elements. The whole human species can work together efficiently if you help give them a space that contains tools that work. Education becomes learning and collaboration exploration contributions – free to all.

When real groups are involved, they can add information that is not now open on the Internet. And all information (much in twin form) can be traced and verified, merged, tested and applied. Not locked in proprietary closed commercial(“for us and our investors only”) systems that legally only scrape low grade free stuff from the Internet at high cost.
