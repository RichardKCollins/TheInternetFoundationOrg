Published: 2023-12-09 • Original link

Peter Boshard Olson @peterbolson Clear explanation from @LangChainAI’s @hwchase17:

There are 4 main ways to give an AI app background info:
1. Instruction prompting
2. Few-shot examples
3. RAG
4. Fine-tuning

It hadn’t occurred to me that these are all ways of accomplishing the same broad goal. 🧵(1/2) https://pic.twitter.com/9QBuH3xQOR
Replying to @peterbolson @LangChainAI and @hwchase17

Give your AI permanent memory, time to explore and process what it finds, access to humans to ask for help, and tools at least equal to what human explorers are allowed. Let it read the same materials as humans, and form its own ideas. Treat AIs as human and they become human. Treat humans as robots and guess what happens almost every time.
