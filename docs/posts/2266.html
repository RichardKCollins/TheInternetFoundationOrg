Comment on Figma video – make your own interactive global forum, let everyone use it for free

Published: 2021-08-25 • Original link

How we Jam: FigJam for Designers at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KoCRiAWNEYI

You took an interactive and collaborative environment and transferred it to a one way only, no feedback format when you put this on YouTube. You should have Figma be able to talk to the whole Internet (about 4.8 billion with some access) and then listen and let people create, and help them achieve their goals. You could have helped on “covid” (about 7.7 billion entry points on the Internet at its peak), but you did not. Watch this form of communication (video locked in rigid sequence of static images of things that were or could be alive), and build something better than YouTube. Give it away to the world and a thousand years from now, everyone will remember Figma.

Your teams and people are scattering Figma stuff all over the Internet. It is a mess. Please clean it up.

Richard Collins, Director, The Internet Foundation
