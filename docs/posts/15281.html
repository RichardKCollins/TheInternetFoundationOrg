Published: 2024-05-16 • Original link

Can the Google AI search for a topic in all human languages and all domain specific languages (DSL)? So concepts or methods in one DSL can be found in different contexts or many different scales and situations? For instance, the models for neutron stars also work for gluon equation of state in accelerators and cosmic rays. Models and methods in many domains can be solved by goal seeking for any combination of constraints.
