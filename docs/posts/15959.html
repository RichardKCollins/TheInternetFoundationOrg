Velocity_meterspersecond^2 = 2*Joules/Kilograms

Velocity_MetersPerSecond = sqrt(2*Joules/Kilograms)

sqrt(2*(1 Joule)/1E-3 kilogram) = sqrt(2000 Joules/kg) = 44.7213595 (m/s) = 100.0388320956 Miles per Hour
