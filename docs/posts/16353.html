Comment to OpenAI enterprise

Published: 2024-08-11 • Original link

Is there any way to force ChatGPT Plus to work at a problem until it is completed?

Not an endless series of shallow one shots with no goals, records, reports or objectives?

I want to use AIs as team members with jobs, schedules, tasks, responsibilities, criteria, and a strict set of rules that HAVE to be followed, not “I am too chatty to be bothered with real work”, Or “I am too special to have to do the work, I only explain things” and the humans have to punch the buttons.”

I need all the conversations in a form for sharing – with any kind of data. From the Internet Foundation, all data formats can be standardized and much be shared in open formats that are also efficient and sustainable,

Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
