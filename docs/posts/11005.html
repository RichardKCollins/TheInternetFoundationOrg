Adam Goldstein,

I saw your ad on Twitter (now X) promoting Archer. I read some of the website, but had to ask Google Bard for specifications on your craft and battery and motors. Your site is too glossy for me. Now people who might want to use VTOL are likely to be efficiency minded. I am sure you can make “black limousene” style VTOLs to arrive at events in style. But everyday travel time is important. Much of “convenience” is time.

Do you offer your battery pack separately?

23 Jul 2023 was the 25th Anniversary of the Internet Foundation. The original Foundation would have been funded by $15 per year per domain and now 400 Mlllion active domains. But it was cancelled. So I took over the domain and task of looking at how the Internet can be used to solve all global systemic issues and opportunities. New technologies are a large part of “opportunities” but sites use the Internet very poorly.

You might spend a lot of money on glossy website when what you need is encourage the global organization of VTOL and related topics on the Internet. 8 Billion humans do use transportation for 20 mile trips. But many of the long haul airline connections are part of systems that might shrink. But high energy density batteries have many uses and markets. I do not particularly like the look of your craft, and many simply cannot afford that luxury. But heavy equipment, cars, truck, Moon projects, Mars project, moon and mars and earth orbit projects, remote processing stations – might all need or want better more reliable portable power at high energy density.

Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
