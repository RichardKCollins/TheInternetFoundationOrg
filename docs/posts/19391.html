If you go back to when Humans were just starting to go off their planet (now) and very diverse groups were learning to cooperate across age groups, languages, philosophies, skills and ways of life – all tackling faster than light communication, gravitational engineering, global lossless AI networks, global governance, atomic and sub-nuclear methods. That same open coordination between groups, and focus on what truly matters – was the reason humans left their planet in the first place. To explore and record every tiny voxel of the Heliosphere, and the whole universe — so they would be ready — for anything.
