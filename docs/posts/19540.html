Fair representation from Senators and Representatives – who actually work for the whole country

Could the United States run more fairly if all Senators and Representatives, and appointees were not allowed to benefit their own state? Where everyone worked for the country as a whole? If states were assigned at random and everyone did their best, the good ones could help weak states, and bad ones in good states would show up. It is not a complete concept, but it seems worth investigating.

Also if there were Representatives that anyone could register on the Internet to follow and have as their Representative, it would not matter if one lived in a red or blue state and never has a real choice of who represents them. If I had to ask a question about the government, a Representative who actually did that as a job and did not party all the time would be preferable.
