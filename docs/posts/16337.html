Feedback to Copilot

The “learn more” are all PDF downloads. but I want to see the context. The hover data on these links is NOT selectable and cannot be copied. One of them says “AC Measurement of magnetic susceptibility” and my only way to search for that title is to manually type it. There IS a department and site for each of those papers and things Copilot found. That is more important in a preliminary discussion of a topic like magnetic permeability – than a few papers which have to be downloaded. and then manually traced. So it says “courses.physics.illinois.edu but no easy way to navigate to that resource. Context and groups are often more important than a PDF paper. A mature professional will collect and organize resources, not just factoids and isolated papers.
