Note to Bard about electromagnetic launch facilities using 3D field methods

Published: 2023-08-10 • Original link

I design an electromagnetic field launch facility that lift the Starship second stage to its mission height and velocity on the planned time schedule ( a few minutes). It completely eliminates or replaces the booster. More importantly it also can catch and gently set the returning craft back in its cradle. So the overall size of the second stage fuel is reduced, since it need not carry “return fuel for orbit to earth”.

The preliminary design which is generously over-designed is 36 GW. I found ways to store that much energy sufficient to give the needed power for a safe lift and reentry. For cargo loads, those can use higher accelerations, so they are more efficient and some of those can simply be inserted directly into orbit. The field can monitor and accelerate all the way from earth, and the support network of tracking sensors and onboard sensors allow for real time path and velocity optimization to minimize sound, lateral accelerations and vibration of the cargo and passengers.

Two such facilities can send cargo or passenger craft from one place to another on earth (Moon Mars etc) so no engines or fuel are required at all. It is all electromagnetic so many new computer, modeling and field methods can be recorded, studied and continuously optimized.

I am setting up a global open collaborative worksite so that any one who wants to learn, or be involved can contribute ideas and work with the core field algorithms. The purpose is to lay a path of continuous improvement using vast untapped human capabilities. There are literally billions of humans who have no proper resources for learning, not jobs. And hundreds of millions trained, but underemployed now.

What I would like to do is train AIs to manage and facilitate the “global open collaborative worksite”. A new class of Internet site specifically designed for efficient global collaborations in Science Technologies Engineering Mathematics Computing Finance Governance and Organizations (STEMCFGO).

The AIs skills and performance can be verified, tested, certified and randomly audited. The site design itself allows for continuous improvement. It can easily adapt to the needs of global lifetime learners, so humans and AIs can work there, can form startups and new ventures. All have $Thanks and crowdfunding, links to short run fabrication, testing and short run manufacturing. It will have open research tools with AI “staff” trained to take over the tedious parts of human research.

When members of the community design systems, products, materials, methods, services that can be used by many humans and projects, the accounting is handled by “AI Staff” so that even “nano contributions” ( a made up term to mean a tiny bit of human effort) can be added. If a person works for free on a project that eventually generated large revenues, they will be paid according to the contribution to the project. And the algorithms for rewards will NOT be controlled by a few humans, rather “continuously improved AI-human efforts” that are open global and auditable.
