Observatories and big science must share with all humans, not just a few, 20%

Published: 2025-01-24 • Original link

https://x.com/RichardKCollin2/status/1882723437590053099

The barriers to use any live or recorded raw data from observatories on the Internet are insurmountably high for everyone. A few 100 thousands insiders is not “everyone”, it is only a tiny fraction of the 8.2 Billion humans now.

There are about 5.4 Billion humans using the Internet. About 2 Billions kids from 4 to 24 learning for the first time. And about 800 Million over 65 in the world. And, 2.8 Billion with no Internet.

All the wonderful stuff in the sky and most of the human species is left with someone else hogging the eyepiece. A few take pictures and share only what they are interested in. The bigger the organization, the worse it gets.

Science Technology Engineering Mathematics now cannot survive without Computers and Internet. So, at least, STEMCI.

A cheap (relative to millions spent on “big”) telescope with a schedule of viewing – day and night (the sun too, the moon too, the sky too, the planets too). can be streamed and recorded in lossless format.

A few live channels and archives globally and that could help serve hundreds of millions, or billions, not just a few. It is lossless raw data that comes closer to science (STEMCI) than just more pretty pictures.

Require all observatories (big science, justified on public funding) to give 20% back to the whole human species, not just a few entitled or close ones.
