All domain specific languages in all human languages can be losslessly simplified.

Published: 2024-12-11 • Original link

Replying to https://x.com/marinkazitnik/status/1866666246726468080

All domain specific languages in all human languages can be losslessly simplified.

Marinka Zitnik, It is not the AIs who struggle, it is the humans behind the green curtains (reference to wizard of oz) who try to make them look intelligent and witty and engaging to keep people coming back.
 
But no isolated, untraceable finite group of fiddlers in the background will get out of their closed groups to actually work with the people who use the languages. And the ones who are affected by the ones who use the languages. Doctors and closed health systems talking more efficient jargon to each other will never make such systems transparent, if the ones who are supposed to be served are deliberately excluded.
 
The Internet and the world does not need more mechanisms to create closed systems. All human languages also means all 8.2 Billion humans and (if we can, listen and understand) the voices of all other life, and complex processes.
 

Richard Collins, The Internet Foundation
