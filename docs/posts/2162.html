Comment on video Colorful sunrise / convection at sunset – 25 June 2020

Published: 2021-08-08 • Original link

UND Atmospheric Sciences – https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eVvaJnSsri0

No numbers? No radar correlations? No models? No satellite correlations? No corrections for sun angle and lighting? No temperatures or pressures or wind speeds? No science? I expected more from “Atmospheric Sciences”. At least put two cameras and do a little 3D estimation. Frame to frame correlations and velocities? Estimate vorticity? Something? Quiet days are good for study of processes when things are not as complex or fast. Ordinary clouds, ordinary reflections and absorptions, ordinary rise and fall, accelerations and cooling and heating. No commentary? At least track some shadows and estimate some angles and velocities.
