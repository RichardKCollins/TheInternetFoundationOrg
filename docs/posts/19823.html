With sources of electromagnetic radiation (energy sources) in a volume of space, patterns of light might occur that are not very likely. “should not exist” can also mean “given the circumstances, very unusual, take note”. A diffractive optical element like a hologram or diffraction grating can turn a simple laser source into something completely unconnected to the scene. It usually means there are micro or nano scale elements causing those things to appear. Even a simple fluorescent material can turn ultraviolet into unusual lights and colors not usually visible. I remember taking a tour of a cave one time nearly 70 years ago where they turned out the lights (completely dark) and then turned on a UV source and the materials in the cave fluoresced in beautiful and somewhat eerie colors. The same phenomena also works with sound. There the shear, longitudinal and transverse waves can interact with human invisible structures to produce sounds that human can hear. Spatial frequencies or temporal frequencies.
