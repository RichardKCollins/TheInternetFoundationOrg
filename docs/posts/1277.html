Google put their ad between my video and its title.

Published: 2021-05-08 • Original link

Please do not put your advertisements between my video and the title of my video. If you have to advertise your work, put it at the top of the screen. and give people the option to remove it. You are effectively using my work to advertise your page. You own the site and I have no recourse. But courtesy says don’t put your ad inside my top content. There is lots of room on the right side. If you want me to help you with covid, I have a project for the whole Internet. And I would like Googles help. But so far, all you seem capable of is these fits and starts of things. Covid needs an Internet wide response, and that means Google scale tools and resources. But you won’t get good will if you arbitrarily do things . This feedback form is not good. Don’t write in the text area. Leave it blank. Make it moveable and resizable. Watch my video that you got in the middle of and it talks about Googles responsibility regarding things like covid and nonprofits.

Richard K Collins, Director, The Internet Foundation
RichardCollins@TheInternetFoundation.Org
