Currently: Known as [[electromagnetic radiation]], these waves occur at various wavelengths to produce a [[Electromagnetic spectrum|spectrum]] of radiation from [[radio wave]]s to [[gamma ray]]s.

Today there is theoretical research on waves with frequencies from quectoHertz (1E-30 Hertz) to QuettaHertz (1E30 Hertz). Practical measurements are roughly from nanoHertz (1E-9 Hertz) to YottaHertz (1E24 Hertz). The rate of development is fast, but Maxwell helps keep it in perspective.

Why: On the Internet, the range of research is broad. Research and development and technologies change weekly. “radio” does not really convey the range any more

Wikipedia has no goal to share knowledge in usable form, or to advance and encourage. So sharing is minimal, there are no tools, the links to active communities are not maintained, and the selections are biased by what volunteers know and are interested in. A better sharing method is possible on the Internet, in groups and for the benefit and survival of the human and related species.
